Problem

Delivery executives, technicians, vendors, domestic workers and visitors repeatedly visit premises, yet there is no intelligent system to recognise recurring visitors and maintain a real-time history of their presence. During incidents, manually reviewing CCTV footage is slow.

Primary module

{{ primary_module }} detects and tracks people, recognises recurring individuals through person re-identification, records time-stamped entry/exit, duration, zone and visible objects, flags anomalies, and lets authorised users search history and auto-generate incident timelines.

Privacy first

Faces and bodies are blurred by default, face recognition is off unless people are enrolled with consent, data is retained for a configurable period, and every sensitive access is audit-logged. Designed with India's DPDP Act 2023 in mind. See PRIVACY.md.

Legacy module

The original {{ legacy_module }} (litter and waste-disposal violation detection) remains available under its own tab and is no longer the focus of development.
